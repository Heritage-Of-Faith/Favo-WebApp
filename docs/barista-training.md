# FAVO Café — Barista Quick Guide

Everything you need to take your first order. Read it once; it takes about five minutes. Screenshots are coming in a later version.

## Logging in

- Open the POS on the iPad at `favo.hofmi.org/pos`.
- You'll see **Enter your PIN**. Tap in your 4-digit PIN, then tap **GO**.
- Wrong PIN? It clears so you can try again.
- Forgot your PIN? Ask the shift manager — they sign in separately.

## Taking an order

- **Add a customer (optional).** Tap the search bar at the top and type a name or phone number. Tap the customer when they appear. No account? Just leave it blank — it's a walk-in. They can link an account later in the customer app.
- **Build the order.** Tap an item on the menu grid. A panel slides up with any **add-ons** (extra shot, oat milk, and so on). Tap the ones you want, then tap **Add to order**. The total updates on its own.
- **Change a quantity.** Use the **+** and **–** buttons next to each line in the order summary. Tap the bin icon to remove a line.
- **Loyalty.** If the customer has enough points, a **Redeem 100 pts (R20 off)** button appears on the payment screen. Tap it there — not while building.

> **Staff free coffee:** Baristas get one free cappuccino per weekday. You apply it from the order **after** it's placed — open the order in the queue and tap **Apply staff discount** (it fills in your own ID). Cappuccinos only, weekdays only.

## Confirming and payment

- Tap **Place Order**. The payment screen opens.
- For a normal order, tap **Charge card — R…**. The secure Yoco card form appears. The customer taps, swipes, or presents their card.
- When the card confirms, the order moves into the live queue **on its own** — you don't tap anything else.
- If the order is free (loyalty or staff discount), there's no card step — just tap **Confirm — send to queue**.

## Working the queue

The right panel shows every live order. Work each one top to bottom:

- Tap **Start Making** when you begin the drink.
- Tap **Mark Ready** when it's done. The customer gets a notification on their phone.
- Tap **DONE — Collected** when they pick it up. The order closes.

## Going offline

> **Offline mode:** If the iPad loses internet, a yellow bar appears at the top: **Working offline — 2 orders pending · Will sync automatically when connection returns**. You can keep working normally.

- Build and **Place Order** exactly as usual.
- On the payment screen you'll see **No connection — Take payment on the Yoco card machine, then tap Confirm**.
- Take the payment on the card machine, then tap **Confirm — paid in person**.
- Orders save on the iPad. When the internet comes back, they send themselves and the pending count drops.
- **Do not refresh or close the page while offline** — wait for the count to reach zero first.
- To see what's still waiting, tap the connectivity pill in the top bar — it opens the **sync** list.

## Stock warnings

- A **yellow** badge on an item means stock is low — you can still sell it.
- A **red** badge means out of stock — it's locked and can't be added.
- Tell the manager when something runs low. They update stock in the admin panel.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Payment fails on the card machine | Try again. If it fails twice, take cash and note it in the order comments. |
| Order stuck in the queue | Open the order and tap **Cancel order**. This only works while it's still in **Ordered**. |
| Screen frozen | Reload with `⌘R`. Any offline orders on the iPad are safe. |
| Can't find a customer | Leave it as a walk-in. They can link their account later in the customer app. |
| Wrong item on an order | You can't edit a placed order. Cancel it and place a new one. |

## End of shift

- Nothing to close off — the system records everything for you.
- Before you leave, tap the connectivity pill in the top bar and check the sync list is empty. If anything is still pending, tell the manager.
