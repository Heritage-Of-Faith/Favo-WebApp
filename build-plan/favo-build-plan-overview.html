The shape of it

Week 1 — Stop, then clear

Close the security gaps that are live right now, ship a real deploy path, delete everything v7 forbids outright, and lay the one database migration everything in Week 2 depends on.

Week 2 — Rebuild, then trade

Rebuild the free weekday cup so it actually reaches office staff, make the money and cost numbers honest, wire a real scheduler, then run two real weekdays of live service before a signed go/no-go.
